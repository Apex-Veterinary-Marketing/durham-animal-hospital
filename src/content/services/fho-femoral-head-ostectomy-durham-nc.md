This surgery involves removing the femoral head, the ball portion of the hip joint, to eliminate bone-on-bone contact and chronic discomfort.</p><p>FHO is commonly recommended for dogs and cats suffering from hip dysplasia, fractures, dislocations, or degenerative joint disease that cannot be managed with medical therapy. By removing the damaged femoral head, the body forms a false joint of scar tissue and muscle, allowing pets to regain mobility and lead an active life without the pain of a diseased hip joint.</p><p>This procedure is most effective for small to medium-sized dogs and cats, but can also benefit larger pets in specific situations.
