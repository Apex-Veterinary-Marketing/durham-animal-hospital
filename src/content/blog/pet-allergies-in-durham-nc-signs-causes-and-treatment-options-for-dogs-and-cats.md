## Pet Allergies in Durham NC

Many people are surprised to learn that dogs and cats can suffer from allergies just like humans do. In fact, pet allergies are one of the most common reasons animals visit the veterinarian, especially during the warmer months here in North Carolina.

If your pet seems uncomfortable, is constantly scratching, or is experiencing recurring skin or ear issues, allergies may be the underlying cause. Understanding the signs, causes, and available treatment options can help keep your pet comfortable and healthy year-round.

### What Causes Pet Allergies in Durham NC?

An allergy occurs when a pet’s immune system overreacts to something in their environment. While humans often experience allergies with sneezing and watery eyes, pets tend to show allergy symptoms on their skin and in their ears.

In Durham, several common environmental factors can trigger allergic reactions in pets, including:

-   Tree pollen
-   Grass pollen
-   Weed pollen
-   Mold spores
-   Dust mites
-   Flea bites
-   Environmental irritants

North Carolina’s warm climate and long growing season create ideal conditions for environmental allergens. As a result, many pets experience allergy symptoms for several months each year, and some may struggle year-round.

### Common Signs of Allergies in Dogs

One of the biggest misconceptions I encounter is that excessive scratching is simply normal behavior. While every dog scratches occasionally, persistent itching often indicates an underlying problem.

Some of the most common allergy symptoms in dogs include:

#### Excessive Scratching

Dogs with allergies often scratch constantly, especially around their face, neck, chest, belly, and legs.

#### Licking or Chewing Their Paws

Many allergic dogs develop itchy feet and will spend significant time licking, chewing, or biting at their paws. Some owners notice brown or reddish staining between the toes from chronic licking.

#### Recurrent Ear Infections

Ear infections are frequently linked to allergies. If your dog experiences repeated ear infections, redness, odor, or head shaking, allergies may be contributing to the problem.

#### Red, Inflamed Skin

Allergies can cause skin irritation, redness, hot spots, and areas of inflammation.

#### Hair Loss

Chronic scratching and licking can lead to thinning fur or noticeable patches of hair loss.

#### Skin Infections

When the skin barrier becomes damaged from ongoing irritation, bacteria and yeast can take advantage of the situation, resulting in secondary infections.

### Common Signs of Allergies in Cats

Cats can also suffer from environmental allergies, although their symptoms may appear somewhat differently than those seen in dogs.

Watch for:

-   Excessive grooming
-   Hair loss
-   Scabs or skin lesions
-   Scratching around the face and neck
-   Ear irritation
-   Overgrooming of the belly or legs
-   Increased shedding
-   Skin redness or irritation

Because cats are naturally meticulous groomers, allergy symptoms can sometimes go unnoticed until significant hair loss or skin irritation develops.

### Why Durham Pets Are Especially Susceptible During Summer

Living in Durham offers many benefits for pets and their owners. We enjoy beautiful parks, walking trails, outdoor activities, and a climate that allows pets to spend plenty of time outside.

Unfortunately, these same environmental conditions can increase exposure to allergens.

During the spring and summer months, pollen counts often rise significantly throughout the area. Grass growth accelerates, flowering plants become more active, and mold thrives in our warm, humid conditions.

Pets that spend time outdoors may come into direct contact with these allergens. Even indoor pets are not completely protected since pollen and environmental particles can enter homes through open doors, windows, clothing, and air circulation systems.

This is one reason many pet owners notice symptoms worsening at certain times of the year.

### Flea Allergies Are More Common Than You Think

One important type of allergy deserves special attention: flea allergy dermatitis.

Some pets are highly sensitive to flea saliva. In these cases, a single flea bite can trigger intense itching and inflammation.

Many owners assume they would easily spot fleas if they were present, but that is not always the case. Pets can develop significant allergic reactions even when flea populations are small or difficult to detect.

Because fleas remain active for much of the year in North Carolina, consistent flea prevention is one of the most important steps pet owners can take to protect their pets.

### How Allergies Affect Your Pet’s Quality of Life

When people think about allergies, they often think of mild discomfort. However, untreated allergies can significantly impact your pet’s overall well-being.

Imagine feeling itchy every day with no way to explain what’s wrong.

Many pets with chronic allergies experience:

-   Constant discomfort
-   Difficulty relaxing
-   Interrupted sleep
-   Recurring infections
-   Skin pain and irritation
-   Behavioral changes
-   Reduced activity levels

Over time, untreated allergies can lead to more severe skin conditions and recurring medical issues that become increasingly difficult to manage.

The good news is that many pets experience significant improvement when allergies are properly addressed.

### Treatment Options for Pet Allergies in Durham NC

The best treatment plan depends on your pet’s specific symptoms, medical history, and the severity of their condition.

At Durham Animal Hospital, we take a comprehensive approach to allergy management. Treatment recommendations may include:

#### Medications to Control Itching

Several safe and effective medications can help reduce itching and inflammation, allowing your pet to feel more comfortable while protecting their skin from further damage.

#### Treatment for Ear Infections

If allergies have contributed to ear infections, addressing both the infection and the underlying allergy is often necessary for long-term relief.

#### Management of Skin Infections

Secondary bacterial or yeast infections frequently accompany allergies and may require treatment to help the skin heal properly.

#### Flea Prevention

Year-round flea prevention is an essential component of allergy management for many pets.

#### Specialized Shampoos and Skin Care Products

Certain medicated shampoos and skin treatments can help soothe irritated skin and remove allergens that may be trapped in the coat.

#### Environmental Management

Simple changes at home can sometimes help reduce allergen exposure. Depending on your pet’s situation, recommendations may include more frequent bathing, wiping paws after outdoor activities, or adjustments to household cleaning routines.

### When Should You Call Your Veterinarian?

It’s a good idea to schedule an examination if your pet is:

-   Scratching excessively
-   Licking or chewing their paws frequently
-   Developing recurring ear infections
-   Losing hair
-   Developing skin redness or hot spots
-   Showing signs of discomfort
-   Experiencing recurring skin infections

The earlier allergies are identified and managed, the easier it is to prevent more serious complications from developing.
