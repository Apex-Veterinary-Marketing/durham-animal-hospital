## Vote for us as your Neighborhood Favorite Veterinarian in Durham!

Nextdoor’s 2020 Neighborhood Favorite Awards – Every year, [Nextdoor](https://nextdoor.com/), the neighborhood hub, hosts local business awards called Neighborhood Favorites where neighbors vote on their favorite local businesses and service providers in a variety of categories. We’re in the running to become a 2020 Neighborhood Favorite, and we’d love your vote!

### Voting runs from February 3-23, 2020. Here’s how to vote:

1\. Head to nextdoor.com/favorites and log in. You’ll be taken directly to the Neighborhood Favorites voting ﬂow.

2\. Click “Start voting” to begin. You can vote for up to 25 categories – vote for us for favorite Pet Care – Veterinarian.

3\. When you’ve ﬁnished voting, spread the word on Nextdoor and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/durhamanimalhospital). Share our custom link to take neighbors directly to our business page to vote: [https://www.nextdoor.com/pages/durham-animal-hospital-durham-nc-1/?neighborhood\_favorites\_survey=true&page\_id=5368193&is=your\_reputation](https://www.nextdoor.com/pages/durham-animal-hospital-durham-nc-1/?neighborhood_favorites_survey=true&page_id=5368193&is=your_reputation)
